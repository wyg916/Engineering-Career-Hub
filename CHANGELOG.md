## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-31

### Added

- Offline Electron desktop app for browsing engineering experience cards.
- Chinese full-text search with project, domain, tag, value, status, and date
  filters.
- Readable card view, project and domain exploration, interview practice, and
  spaced repetition.
- Local favorites, notes, confidence, review state, and JSON backup/restore.
- Light and dark themes, text sizing, wide reading mode, keyboard navigation,
  and Windows high-DPI support.
- Synthetic end-to-end fixture and privacy-safe product screenshots.
